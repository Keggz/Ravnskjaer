Muspelheim was Ben-Gerd's name for the place where the company would reach Eldrbrand's essence. She was uncertain of its exact location on the other side. This entry recounts what Jardar, Trym and Bjarte experienced in the late summer of 794; it does not establish the realm's boundaries or place within a complete cosmology.

## The way beneath the tree

Ben-Gerd's poisonous brew put them into a trance. They awoke in a dark chamber with an enormous tree, entered the opening at its root and found nine doors. Bjarte was drawn towards the third, the door of fire. Jardar felt drawn to the seventh, but followed the others. Only the third was opened.

## Lava, altars and a throne

A long paved road crossed a landscape of heat, ash and lava. Small fire beings gathered on rocks and patches of land beside the road. Several tremors marked the journey; the accounts link the first to Eldrbrand's name being spoken at Ravnskjær.

At the farthest point they reached stood a great flame between three altar towers arranged in a triangle. A throne stood farther in, with lava falls behind it. Jardar gave something personal connected to Gyda, Bjarte smashed his tower in anger, and Trym passed a trial of occult knowledge. Eldrbrand's figure then emerged from the flame, and they fought his essence.

## The escape

As the battle turned against Eldrbrand, the fire beings grew restless, the ground shook and the lava falls swelled. The three fled. A vast figure at the falls was thought possibly to be Surtr, but no certain identification has been recorded.

They awoke in Ben-Gerd's hut and received the antidote from Eadgifu. The state of the place after they left is unknown.
