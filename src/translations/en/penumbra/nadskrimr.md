Náðskrímr took possession of Trellen Tor. During the winter journey to Skardvik in 793–794, the company found him standing naked and covered in ice in the blizzard, apparently untouched by the cold. Something spoke through him and said that the man they sought was no longer there.

## The cold in Skardvik

When Trym sensed the presence and the company encountered Tor, Trym collapsed. Cold, dead bodies rose around them and attacked. Trym was left with a cold within him that the fire could not drive out.

## Solveig's ritual

The company captured Tor and carried him into the longhouse. Solveig called the being «the spirit of frost» and Náðskrímr during the ritual she performed. She sacrificed her mortally wounded father, Åskell, and used his heart to break its grip on Tor.

The attacking dead immediately collapsed. Tor and Trym briefly awoke, but remained severely weakened during the journey home and for several nights afterwards.

## What is still unknown

The account does not establish how Tor first fell into the spirit's grip, why Skardvik was struck, or what became of Náðskrímr after the ritual. The ending of the attack is not, in itself, proof that the spirit was destroyed.
