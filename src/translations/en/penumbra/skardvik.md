Skardvik has still not been cleared after the attack. The dead were carried out of the longhouse, but never received the burial the company promised them. No one else was sent to do the work.

This leaves its mark on the place's Penumbra. The attack ended, but Skardvik has found no peace. Death and the unfulfilled promise cling to the place on the other side.

How this appears to those who enter the Penumbra has not yet been recorded. No particular spirit or new threat is confirmed here.
