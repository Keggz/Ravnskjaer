Eldrbrand is known as a jötunn. He is a powerful spirit who manifested physically and could intervene in the human world.

## Traces in the story

Eldrbrand is associated with the destruction of [Steinvik](/en/places/steinvik), the place Jardar came from. Vilmar named him as Steinvik's destroyer when the company met the wanderer in Vatnaborg.

He also attacked [Ravnskjær](/en/places/ravnskjaer). [Bjørn Ulvsson](/en/characters/bjorn-ulvsson) died during the attack, and [Ben-Gerd](/en/characters/ben-gerd) lost her life protecting Asgeir.
