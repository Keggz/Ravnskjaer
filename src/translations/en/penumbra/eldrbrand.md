Eldrbrand is known as a jotunn, a powerful spirit who manifested physically and could act in the human world.

## Steinvik and Ravnskjær

Eldrbrand is connected to the destruction of [Steinvik](/en/places/steinvik), Jardar's home. Vilmar called him Steinvik's destroyer when the company met the wanderer in Vatnaborg.

In the late summer of 794, he attacked [Ravnskjær](/en/places/ravnskjaer). Those who faced him described a creature some thirty metres tall, surrounded by smoke, fire and ash. He was far faster than the defenders expected, and his stones destroyed much of the settlement. Ben-Gerd died saving Asgeir; Bjørn was mortally wounded during the defence.

## The body and the essence

Ben-Gerd believed Eldrbrand had to be fought simultaneously in the physical world and where his essence dwelt. Jardar, Trym and Bjarte drank her poisonous brew and reached a landscape of fire and lava, which she called [Muspelheim](/en/penumbra/places/muspelheim).

After trials at three altar towers, they encountered Eldrbrand's essence as a figure in a great flame. All three were badly wounded, but defeated the essence. Trym called upon Náðskrímr during the battle and had to drive the frost spirit out of himself again.

## The fall

On the physical side, Vilmar's jet of water knocked Eldrbrand over. An opening appeared in his chest, and Saia climbed up and drove her sword into it. His body disappeared after his defeat. The ground where he had fallen remained black, ruined and hot.

The three travellers fled as the landscape of fire shook and grew violent. They thought they glimpsed Surtr at the lava falls; the identity is unconfirmed. Eadgifu gave them the antidote when they returned to Ben-Gerd's hut.

## What remains unknown

The spirit's origin and the way he took physical form have not been explained. The disappearance of his body and defeat of his essence do not establish his ultimate fate. What he and Ben-Gerd may have said to one another before she died is unknown.
