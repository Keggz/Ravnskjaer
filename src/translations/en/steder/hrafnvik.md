Hrafnvik lies north of [Ravnskjær](/en/places/ravnskjaer).

It is the home of the twins Ulrik den Lille and Alrik den Store. After Eldrbrand's attack, new warriors were chosen for Ravnskjær's hird based on the summer games. The twins were among those selected.

Torstein wanted to strengthen ties with Hrafnvik as part of his efforts to build alliances and improve defences before and during the winter of 794–795.

[Read about the new hird warriors in “After Eldrbrand”](/en/timeline/etter-eldrbrand#new-hands-in-the-hird).

Further details about the settlement have yet to be recorded.
