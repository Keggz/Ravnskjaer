Askvin lies east of [Ravnskjær](/en/places/ravnskjaer).

It is the home of the axe warrior Ymril Faunsdatter. After Eldrbrand's attack, new warriors were chosen for Ravnskjær's hird based on the summer games. Ymril Faunsdatter was among them.

Torstein wanted to strengthen ties with Askvin as part of his efforts to build alliances and improve defences before and during the winter of 794–795.

[Read about the new hird warriors in “After Eldrbrand”](/en/timeline/etter-eldrbrand#new-hands-in-the-hird).

Further details about the settlement have yet to be recorded.
