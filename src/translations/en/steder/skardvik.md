Skardvik lies north of Ravnskjær. Before the attack, about fifty people lived here, in smaller houses around a longhouse. The village was led by Åskell Helgesson.

## The siege in the winter of 793–794

A figure had been seen around the village before dead inhabitants began to rise and attack others. The survivors barricaded themselves in the longhouse and sent for help.

Six warriors from Ravnskjær arrived through the winter weather. They found Trellen Tor in Náðskrímr's grip. During the battle, Solveig sacrificed her mortally wounded father Åskell to complete the ritual that made the dead collapse.

[Read the full winter journey to Skardvik](/en/timeline/vinterferden-til-skardvik).

## Unburied dead and an unfulfilled promise

The survivors cleared the longhouse, gathered supplies and accompanied the company to Ravnskjær after one final night. The dead were left outside the houses. The plan was to return in milder weather to give them a proper burial.

This was never done. Nor has the company sent anyone else to clear the site and bury them. What remains unresolved is also tied to [Skardvik on the other side](/en/penumbra/places/skardvik).
