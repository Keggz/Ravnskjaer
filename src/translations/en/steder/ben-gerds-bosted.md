Ben-Gerd lived in a cabin in the forest outside Ravnskjær. People sought her advice when grief or fear became too heavy to bear. She never charged them.

## After Ben-Gerd

Ben-Gerd died during Eldrbrand's attack while protecting Asgeir. Her home remains associated with her memory.
