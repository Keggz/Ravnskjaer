Ben-Gerd lived in a cabin in the forest outside Ravnskjær. People sought her advice when grief or fear became too heavy to bear. She never charged them.

## After Ben-Gerd

Ben-Gerd died during Eldrbrand's attack while protecting Asgeir. Her home remains associated with her memory.

## The three who awoke here

During Eldrbrand's attack in 794, Jardar, Trym and Bjarte lay in a trance after drinking Ben-Gerd's brew. When they returned from fighting the jotunn's essence, they awoke in the hut. Eadgifu cared for them and gave them the antidote. By then Ben-Gerd had lost her life outside Ravnskjær.

## A presence afterwards

After the battle, Eadgifu said the hut did not feel empty. What she sensed has not been explained, and no particular spirit or figure has been identified.

## Laughter in the winter rumours

During the winter of 794–795, there were rumours of laughter from the hut. These are recorded as rumours, not as confirmation that Ben-Gerd had returned.
