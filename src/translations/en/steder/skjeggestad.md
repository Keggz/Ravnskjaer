Skjeggestad lies south of Ravnskjær. Hjalti Urdsson has good connections here through his work with trade routes and barter agreements.

The journey to Skjeggestad is also mentioned in Ravnskjær's preparations and sacrificial rites.

## The fire of 793

Skjeggestad was surrounded by a palisade with watchtowers and one main entrance. The jarl's house and a storehouse stood farthest from the entrance, and Ola Sjørev used a training ground outside the town.

Hjalti reported disappearances and weakened forces. When Jardar, Torstein, Trym, Asgeir and Saia arrived, they posed as mercenaries. They carried valuables out of the town and set it on fire. The inhabitants were trapped when the gate collapsed. Ola and his men were killed outside.

The company examined the ruins and concluded that no one had survived. Skjeggestad was thus destroyed by the time they returned to Ravnskjær.
