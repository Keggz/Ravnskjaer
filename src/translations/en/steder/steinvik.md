Steinvik is the abandoned settlement west of the forest.

## Connections to Ravnskjær

Several people suspect Gunhild Treløse was born here, but her origins are not known for certain.

The group met Herman from Hestvik on the road to Steinvik.

## The visit in 793

The company from Ravnskjær travelled here after seeing smoke in this direction. The settlement had burned down, but the ruins had been cleared and the dead buried. No one was there when the company arrived.

Here, Gyda, Jardar's deceased wife, spoke through Trym. Later, the company met Skjold-Brynjar, Runa Måneveis, Geir Ulvhjart and Yrsa Nattøyne and spoke with them by the fire about the box and the supernatural threats.
