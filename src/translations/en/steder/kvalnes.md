Kvalnes lies north of [Ravnskjær](/en/places/ravnskjaer).

It is the home of the archer Harald Hårmagre. After Eldrbrand's attack, new warriors were chosen for Ravnskjær's hird based on the summer games. Harald Hårmagre was among them.

Torstein wanted to strengthen ties with Kvalnes as part of his efforts to build alliances and improve defences before and during the winter of 794–795.

[Read about the new hird warriors in “After Eldrbrand”](/en/timeline/etter-eldrbrand#new-hands-in-the-hird).

Further details about the settlement have yet to be recorded.
