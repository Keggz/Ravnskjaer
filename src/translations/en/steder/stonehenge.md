Stonehenge is an ancient arrangement of large standing stones in southern England.

The stone circle is one of the places in the campaign world outside Norway.

## The second westward voyage in 793

The company came here after following Trym's pull and obtaining directions in Vatnaborg. They examined the stone formations and found signs resembling old runes. Jardar managed to open the box a little, without any immediate effect becoming apparent.

Outside the formation itself, they found a wooden hatch and an underground passage leading to a room with a chest. A thin, sinewy creature attacked them when Trym opened it. Jardar was bitten in the neck, and several others suffered serious injuries. The creature vanished before their eyes and escaped. The company linked the later mass deaths in Vatnaborg to this encounter, though they did not witness the attack on the town itself.
