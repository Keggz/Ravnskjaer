Haugnes lies south of [Ravnskjær](/en/places/ravnskjaer).

It is the home of Geir Fieri, who fought with a hand axe. After Eldrbrand's attack, new warriors were chosen for Ravnskjær's hird based on the summer games. Geir Fieri was among them.

Torstein wanted to strengthen ties with Haugnes as part of his efforts to build alliances and improve defences before and during the winter of 794–795.

[Read about the new hird warriors in “After Eldrbrand”](/en/timeline/etter-eldrbrand#new-hands-in-the-hird).

Further details about the settlement have yet to be recorded.
