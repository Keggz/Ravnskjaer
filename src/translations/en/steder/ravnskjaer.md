Ravnskjær is a proud settlement of around 250 souls on Norway's west coast. Farming, fishing, trade and crafts shape daily life, while the hird protects its people and their homes.

## People and rule

Jarl Torstein «Draugfødt» Bjørnson rules following his father Bjørn Ulvsson's death during Eldrbrand's attack. The small council includes representatives from the Ulvsson clan, the Torløv clan, the Helga clan and the Tjugeskalle people. Saia represents the people and the hird.

## Life in the settlement

Ravnskjær is a stopping place for travellers along the coast. Merchants, shipworkers, healers and farming folk live here. Knut En-øye makes tools and weapons, Rolf Bakstein heats the baking oven, and Åsmund Helgason oversees trade and stores.

The harbour, longhouse and workshops are parts of Ravnskjær itself. Over time, the settlement has acquired a ditch and palisade to protect it against greater dangers.
