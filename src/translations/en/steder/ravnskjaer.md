Ravnskjær is a proud settlement of around 250 souls on Norway's west coast. Farming, fishing, trade and crafts shape daily life, while the hird protects its people and their homes.

## People and rule

Jarl Torstein «Draugfødt» Bjørnson rules following his father Bjørn Ulvsson's death during Eldrbrand's attack. The small council includes representatives from the Ulvsson clan, the Torløv clan, the Helga clan and the Tjugeskalle people. Saia represented the people and the hird before moving to the Isle of Wight in 795.

## Life in the settlement

Ravnskjær is a stopping place for travellers along the coast. Merchants, shipworkers, healers and farming folk live here. Knut En-øye makes tools and weapons, Rolf Bakstein heats the baking oven, and Åsmund Helgason oversees trade and stores.

The harbour, longhouse and workshops are parts of Ravnskjær itself. Over time, the settlement has acquired a ditch and palisade to protect it against greater dangers.

## The completed defences

The palisade was completed early in the spring of 794. Much of the nearby woodland had been felled for construction, also giving the guards a clearer view of the ground before Ravnskjær.

## Eldrbrand's attack

In the late summer of 794, most inhabitants were evacuated before Eldrbrand arrived. The defenders remained. The jotunn's stones destroyed roughly half the settlement and half the longhouse. Ben-Gerd and Bjørn died, together with many of the men defending the place.

Asgeir found about fifteen survivors among those who had stayed; this is not a population count for all of Ravnskjær after the attack. The evacuees were called home from Skjeggestad once the battle was over.

Where Eldrbrand fell, his body disappeared, but the ground remained black, ruined and hot. [Read Halvdan's account of the battle](/en/timeline/kampen-mot-eldrbrand). [The funeral and rebuilding follow in the next account](/en/timeline/etter-eldrbrand).

## The autumn and winter afterwards

The people returned to the destruction and gathered for the boat funeral of the dead. Torstein was appointed interim jarl pending the allthing. Ravnskjær was rebuilt before winter, while further work, training and strengthening of the defences continued through the winter of 794–795.

New hird warriors came from Askvin, Hrafnvik, Kvalnes and Haugnes. The final harvests yielded less than expected despite good seed. The cause is unknown. The black ground where Eldrbrand fell remained warm enough for snow to melt slowly.

## The night the blood awoke

Later in 795, Jardar assumed command of the hird and the entire defence, Bjarte took over the guards, and Njoldr became captain of the guard. After the ambush of the Jotunheimen company, Runa Måneveis returned with the box and warned of an attack.

The boats were wrecked, Geir Fieri and a guard were murdered at the harbour, and the civilians sheltered in the longhouse. Fomori broke a gate, and six werewolves entered the battle. As Jardar, Torstein and Bjarte lay dying and the enemy opened the box, they underwent their First Change. With Rolf Bakstein, they drove the enemy away. The losses, rebuilding and subsequent aftermath have not yet been recorded.
