Lindisfarne is an island with a monastery off the coast of Northumbria, across the sea from Norway.

## The voyage from Ravnskjær in 793

The expedition from Ravnskjær landed on a beach with fishing equipment and small boats. After investigating the farms, the company made its way to the settlement and the church on the hill. Many of the monks and scholars were killed in the attack, and the company then defeated eight warriors.

Beneath the church's storeroom, Jardar, Torstein, Asgeir and Saia found a burial chamber. There, Jardar took the box from a large dead man, who awoke along with three other draugar.

The company eventually sailed home with plunder and three people taken as thralls. The accounts of the landing, the attack, the burial chamber and the departure are collected in the chronicle.

## How the name became known

The company did not know the island's name during the first visit. Only afterwards did Halvdan Skrivare discover the name Lindisfarne with the help of Ceadda Gummer, who taught him some English and Latin.
