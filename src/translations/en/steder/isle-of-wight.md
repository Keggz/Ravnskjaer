The campaign notes refer to the island by its modern name. It was the home of the monk Æthelberth, whom the company had rescued from a shipwreck.

They put him ashore here and promised to collect him again. After the journey to the stone circle, Saia returned for him, and he willingly accompanied them to Ravnskjær.

Trym felt a pull inland from the area near the island. This became the starting point for their onward journey on foot towards Vatnaborg and Stonehenge. The small fishing island where the company made a trade agreement is a different, unnamed place.
