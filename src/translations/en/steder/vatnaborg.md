Vatnaborg is the name the company uses for the town they visited on the second westward voyage in 793. No more precise modern location is established in the notes.

## Two very different visits

On the first visit, they followed some bathing soldiers and then approached the guards. They were welcomed warmly, paid for food and lodging, and traded with the locals. Bjarte met Eadgifu here.

The inhabitants said the stone circle was four to six days' walk away. When the company later returned, the town was in chaos after many deaths. The dead were pale and had marks on their necks. The company suspected the creature that had escaped from beneath Stonehenge, but had not witnessed the attack itself.

There were survivors in the town. Eadgifu chose to leave with the company. Trym also met Vilmar Vandreren in a house here.
