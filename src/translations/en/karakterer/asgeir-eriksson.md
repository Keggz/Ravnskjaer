Asgeir is a pathfinder and a warrior in the hird. Ben-Gerd protected him from certain death when Eldrbrand attacked Ravnskjær, losing her own life in the process.

## Responsibilities after the second westward voyage

Before the second westward voyage in 793, Asgeir studied navigation under Hårek. Hårek did not join the voyage itself. Asgeir steered the ship, with Jardar as his deputy and the others helping as needed. Saia led the expedition. During the visit to Vatnaborg, Asgeir traded his bow for a longer, heavier one.

After returning home, he accepted responsibility for the hunting party. Throughout the winter of 793–794, he organised the hunting parties with Brynhild and helped the smith when he could.

## The dream before the voyage

Before the first voyage, Jardar, Saia, Torstein and Asgeir shared a dream with slight variations. [Watch the four surviving versions](/en/dreams-and-omens/#drommen-for-ferden). It has not been established who received which version.

## The battle against Eldrbrand

In the spring and summer of 794, Asgeir worked with Brynhild on hunting and rebuilding Skjeggestad.

When Eldrbrand attacked in late summer, Asgeir stayed with Ravnskjær's defenders. He displayed the box to the jotunn and was pursued. Ben-Gerd threw him clear, but was herself seized and killed.

Asgeir emerged relatively unhurt and searched the ruins. He found Bjørn mortally wounded inside the gate. The jarl met him with a questioning look; Asgeir nodded before Bjørn died. Asgeir counted about fifteen survivors among those who had remained at Ravnskjær, distinct from the inhabitants evacuated before the battle.

## The final journey

About a month after the encounter with the bears outside Ravnskjær, Asgeir and Trym undertook to carry the box to Jotunheimen. Ulrik the Little and Alrik the Great accompanied them as protection. Wolves ambushed the company. Runa Måneveis, who had secretly followed them with the ulfhednar, returned with the box and reported that both Asgeir and Trym had died. Asgeir's exact final moments have not been recorded.

He was formerly a player character. His player left the group between the interrupted Jotunheimen journey and this account.
