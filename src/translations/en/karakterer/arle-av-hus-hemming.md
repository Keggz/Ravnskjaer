Arle was eight years old when Skardvik was attacked in the winter of 793–794. His parents, Bera and Hemming, died defending the longhouse.

## Jardar intervenes

After his parents fell, Arle himself came under attack. Jardar saw what was happening and barely managed to save him. After the battle, Jardar decided to take the boy under his wing.

Arle spoke little after what had happened, but understood that Jardar would look after him. He accompanied the survivors to Ravnskjær.

## A new home

After their return, Jardar cared for Arle and began training him. He protected the boy like the son he had never had.

## The boy who wanted to help Jardar

During the battle for Ravnskjær in 795, Arle ran out of the longhouse to help Jardar against a werewolf. He shouted “I'll help you, Jardar!” and struck the creature. It knocked him hard against the longhouse. What happened to him afterwards belongs to the aftermath, which has not yet been recorded.
