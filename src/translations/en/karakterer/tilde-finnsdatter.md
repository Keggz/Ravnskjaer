Tilde Finnsdatter belongs to the Tjugeskalle clan and is recorded as nineteen years old. She is calm and shy.

## Marriage and child

Tilde married Torstein Bjørnson shortly after the return from the second westward voyage in 793. The marriage was intended to strengthen the ties between the jarl's family and the Tjugeskalle people.

She became pregnant after the wedding. Their child was born in the summer of 794.

## What people say

Some say she has nightmares. The reason is unknown.
