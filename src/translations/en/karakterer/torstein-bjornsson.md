Torstein Bjørnson is jarl of Ravnskjær. He bears the epithet «Draugfødt», Draug-born, because he was stillborn but came to life.

## Family and rule

Torstein is Bjørn Ulvsson's son and took over after his father died during Eldrbrand's attack. He is married to Tilde Finnsdatter.

He rules alongside a small council with representatives from the Torløv clan, the Helga clan, the Tjugeskalle people and the Ulvsson clan. Saia represents the people and the hird.

## Before he became jarl

The second westward voyage in 793 was led by Saia, whom Bjørn chose to avoid the impression of favouring his own son. After returning home, Torstein accepted his father's offer to lead the hird.

Shortly after the return, he married Tilde Finnsdatter. The marriage was intended to strengthen ties with the Tjugeskalle people. Tilde became pregnant after the wedding, and their child was born in the summer of 794.

Before the first voyage, Jardar, Saia, Torstein and Asgeir shared a dream with slight variations. [Watch the four surviving versions](/en/dreams-and-omens/#drommen-for-ferden). It has not been established who received which version.
