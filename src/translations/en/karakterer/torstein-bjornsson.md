Torstein Bjørnson is jarl of Ravnskjær. He bears the epithet «Draugfødt», Draug-born, because he was stillborn but came to life.

## Family and rule

Torstein is Bjørn Ulvsson's son and took over after his father died during Eldrbrand's attack. He is married to Tilde Finnsdatter.

He rules alongside a small council with representatives from the Torløv clan, the Helga clan, the Tjugeskalle people and the Ulvsson clan. Saia represented the people and the hird before moving to the Isle of Wight in 795.

## Before he became jarl

The second westward voyage in 793 was led by Saia, whom Bjørn chose to avoid the impression of favouring his own son. After returning home, Torstein accepted his father's offer to lead the hird.

Shortly after the return, he married Tilde Finnsdatter. The marriage was intended to strengthen ties with the Tjugeskalle people. Tilde became pregnant after the wedding, and their child was born in the summer of 794.

## The dream before the voyage

Before the first voyage, Jardar, Saia, Torstein and Asgeir shared a dream with slight variations. [Watch the four surviving versions](/en/dreams-and-omens/#drommen-for-ferden). It has not been established who received which version.

## The defence of Ravnskjær in 794

When Eldrbrand attacked in the late summer of 794, Torstein fought on the physical side alongside Asgeir, Saia and Ravnskjær's defenders. He tried unsuccessfully to find a way to lure the jotunn into the water.

After Vilmar knocked Eldrbrand over, Torstein tried to leap from his horse onto the jotunn. He could not hold on and was badly injured in the fall. He survived, while his father Bjørn died of his wounds during the attack.

## Interim jarl and the funeral

After the battle, the small council appointed Torstein interim jarl until an allthing could make a formal decision. He ordered the funeral boats prepared. Beside Bjørn he laid a sword his father had given him, and delivered a few hesitant but effective words before Asgeir lit the boats with flaming arrows.

Through the autumn and winter of 794–795, he worked towards alliances and recruiting more warriors from Askvin, Hrafnvik, Kvalnes and Haugnes. Skjeggestad would be allowed to govern and manage its own affairs. The company postponed taking the box to Jotunheimen until winter was over.

## The allthing and the feast in 795

The allthing decided that Torstein should retain his power as jarl. In early summer 795, he gathered Ravnskjær for a feast to raise spirits and strengthen ties between the clans.

During the feast, he publicly accused Øystæinn of unlawful dealings. Later he struck him unconscious from behind, and Øystæinn was bound in Bjarte's house. Saia, Asgeir and Trym objected to the handling of the affair and the risk of division. After Bjarte persuaded Øystæinn to describe the threats near Blodskjæft, Torstein apologised to Åsmund for his conduct.

## The jarl and the bear’s awakening

Later in 795, Torstein entrusted command of the hird and the overall defence to Jardar. He concentrated on his duties as jarl but continued training with the warriors.

During the attack he refused to surrender the box and encouraged the defenders with words of Ravnskjær, their loved ones and Valhalla. When Ulf Blodøye and Harald Hårmagre were struck down and the last guards fled the shield line, he remained. He severed an arm from the great werewolf before him and killed it with his other sword through its throat.

Three more werewolves came behind them, and Torstein was mortally wounded. When the box opened, he felt cold and a call from the earth to stand. He awakened as Gurahl, a huge bear with grey-white fur and frost in its hairs, and fought on alongside Jardar and Bjarte.
