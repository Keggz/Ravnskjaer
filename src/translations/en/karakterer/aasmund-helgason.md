Åsmund Helgason leads the Helga clan. He negotiates with travellers, oversees the village stores and has connections to trade routes in every direction.

## Family and trade

Yrsa is his elder daughter and Runa the younger. Elrik is his adopted son, and Øystæinn «Sølvskeptikeren» Spjótaldr is his younger brother.

Hjalti maps trade routes and arranges barter agreements on his behalf. Halvdan Skrivare works closely with him.
