Ulf Blodøye was a berserker in Ravnskjær's hird. He joined the expedition Bjørn Ulvsson sent west to obtain supplies.

## The last shield line

Ulf stood at the front of the shield formation with Torstein, Jardar, Bjarte and Harald Hårmagre during the battle for Ravnskjær in 795. The largest of the first three werewolves killed him with claws through his head, before Torstein slew the creature.
