Finn Tjugeskalle led the Tjugeskalle people and was responsible for training the hird. Several of the jarl's warriors owed him both their skills and their lives.

He was precise and demanding in his work. Finn is dead.

## Family

Brynhild is his sister. Rask is his eldest son, and Tilde is his daughter.

## Death near Ravnskjær

In the late summer or autumn of 793, Finn followed the hird along the trail of a missing guard. A wolf-creature attacked him, inflicting mortal wounds. Bjarte killed the creature and tried to help Finn, but could not save him.

The creature became a human corpse after death. This made the company's account difficult to substantiate and contributed to the political unrest following Finn's death.
