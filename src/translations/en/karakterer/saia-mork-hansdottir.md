Saia is a shieldmaiden and was part of Ravnskjær's hird. She represented the people and the hird on the small council before settling on the Isle of Wight in 795.

## The second westward voyage and the small council

Bjørn appointed Saia to lead the second westward voyage in 793. He wanted to avoid giving the impression that command was being awarded in favour of his own son Torstein. The appointment caused disagreement within the company, but Saia had the jarl's authority to lead the journey.

Saia spoke on the company's behalf in several encounters with English speakers during the voyage. She convinced the company to spare the bathing soldiers and helped establish friendly contact with Vatnaborg.

She formed a close friendship with the monk Æthelberth and collected him from the Isle of Wight before the journey home. Back in Ravnskjær, she accepted a seat on Bjørn's small council alongside Åsmund, Ragna, Brynhild and Inga.

## The dream before the voyage

Before the first voyage, Jardar, Saia, Torstein and Asgeir shared a dream with slight variations. [Watch the four surviving versions](/en/dreams-and-omens/#drommen-for-ferden). It has not been established who received which version.

## The sword against Eldrbrand

During the attack on Ravnskjær in the late summer of 794, Saia remained with the defenders. She used her riding skills to escape the jotunn and try to keep him away from the settlement.

When Vilmar's jet of water knocked Eldrbrand over and an opening appeared in his chest, she rode in and gained a foothold on his body. She climbed onto his chest and drove her sword into the opening. She climbed down before the jotunn's body disappeared. Her attack formed part of the same struggle as Jardar, Trym and Bjarte's battle against his essence on the other side.

## Trust at the spring feast

When Torstein took Øystæinn captive during the feast in early summer 795, Saia understood that the dispute concerned trust in the leadership. After Bjørn's death, poor harvests and dark rumours, the people needed a leader who appeared reassuring, fair and strong.

She saw the Helga clan at a tipping point: excessive pressure could make them bitter and dangerous, while overly gentle treatment could be seen as favouritism or fear of acting. This lay behind her strong reaction and heated argument with Torstein.

## The wounds of Blodskjæft

During [the journey to Blodskjæft](/en/timeline/da-jegerne-ble-bytte), Saia fought with a sword. She lost an eye and suffered a severe wound across her face. She survived, but was badly injured, like the rest of the company. Which eye she lost has not been specified.

## Departure for the Isle of Wight

After the interrupted journey to Jotunheimen in 795, Saia chose to settle near Æthelberth's home on the Isle of Wight and establish a trading post. With the company's agreement, she sailed with him, Hårek Stjernesyn and some volunteers on one of the boats. Hårek returned with trade goods; Saia and Æthelberth stayed behind. She was therefore not among the defenders in the subsequent battle for Ravnskjær.

Saia, Asgeir and Trym became NPCs between the interrupted Jotunheimen journey and this account, after their players left the group.
