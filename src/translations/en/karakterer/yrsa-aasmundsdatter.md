Yrsa is Åsmund Helgason's eldest daughter. She is ambitious and a scholar of languages.

She writes contracts and keeps trade records for the Helga clan.

## Language studies in 793–794

Yrsa worked with Halvdan Skrivare on English and the interpretation of writings from the first westward voyage. The work progressed slowly. During the winter, she, Halvdan and Æthelberth were among those with whom Trym learned a little Latin.
