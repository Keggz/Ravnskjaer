Runa Måneveis was among the four ulfhednar who met the company from Ravnskjær in Steinvik in 793. They spent the evening and night by the fire discussing the box, the keys and the powers that were stirring.

## The box and the siege of 795

Runa and other ulfhednar secretly followed Trym, Asgeir and the twins towards Jotunheimen to protect them against an expected attack from the Wyrm's servants. After the ambush, Skjold-Brynjar got her away on a horse with the box.

Ten nights after the company's departure she reached Ravnskjær unconscious and badly wounded. Vera and Ragna cared for her. Runa contacted Trym's spirit and reported that Trym and Asgeir had died; the twins' fate was uncertain. She warned the council of the imminent attack.

Before the battle she was back on her feet. She looked into the Penumbra and crossed over to defend Ravnskjær there. Her experiences on the other side during the fighting have not yet been recorded.
