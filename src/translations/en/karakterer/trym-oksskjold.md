Trym is a warrior in the hird with an interest in the occult.

Trym is dead.

## The pull westward and his new duties

On the second westward voyage in 793, the group followed Trym's spiritual pull towards Stonehenge. He was wounded in the hand while opening the chest beneath the stone circle. Later, he met Vilmar Vandreren in Vatnaborg.

After returning home, Trym accepted responsibility for Ravnskjær's spiritual life and rituals. Throughout the winter of 793–794, he often visited Ben-Gerd and began learning a little Latin alongside Æthelberth, Halvdan and Yrsa. This belongs to the time before his later death.

## The cold in Skardvik

During the winter journey to Skardvik in 793–794, Trym sensed a presence before the company encountered Tor in Náðskrímr's grip. Trym collapsed and was carried into the longhouse. He shivered with a cold that the fire could not drive out.

Solveig cared for him. When the ritual against the spirit was complete, both Trym and Tor briefly awoke. Trym survived this journey and returned to Ravnskjær, but remained weakened for several nights afterwards. This happened before his later death.

## Frost against fire

Trym continued studying languages with Halvdan and Yrsa Åsmundsdatter in the spring of 794. In late summer he travelled to the other side with Jardar and Bjarte to fight Eldrbrand's essence. His trial at the altar towers required occult knowledge.

During the battle he called upon Náðskrímr and received temporary strength. The spirit tried to possess him. Trym drove it out, but was nearly killed by the cold. He survived the battle against Eldrbrand, returned to Ben-Gerd's hut and received the antidote from Eadgifu. His later death belongs to another sequence of events.

## The parchment at the spring feast

Through the winter of 794–795, Trym continued learning languages with Halvdan and the people from England, including Latin with Æthelberth. During the feast in early summer 795, Halvdan gave him Parchment Scroll III, attributed to Brother Matthaeus of Lindisfarne.

Trym went through the text with the company. The marks on Øystæinn's neck and the account of servants led him to believe Øystæinn had become a “spawn”. Trym was also among those who objected to Torstein's treatment of him. This happened before Trym's later death.

## The ambush in Blodskjæft

On the journey after the spring feast in 795, Trym stood near the narrow passage behind Jardar, Bjarte and Torstein when a creature dropped onto him from the ceiling. He fought with an axe and was gravely wounded, like the other five members of the company.

The three vampires were beheaded following the knowledge from the parchment. Trym survived and returned to Ravnskjær with the others; his later death did not occur in this battle.

## The final journey and the spirit’s answer

About a month after the bear encounter in 795, Trym and Asgeir set out for Jotunheimen with the box, accompanied by the twins from Hrafnvik. Wolves ambushed them. Runa Måneveis returned with the box ten nights after their departure and later confirmed that Trym and Asgeir had died.

Runa called upon Trym's spirit. He spoke of darkness, cold, wetness, blood and corruption. He remembered attacking wolves and other wolves fighting them, before something pierced his back and everything went dark. He could not say what had happened to the others before contact broke.

He was formerly a player character. His player left the group between the interrupted Jotunheimen journey and this account.
