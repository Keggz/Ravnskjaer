Brynhild is Finn Tjugeskalle's sister. Once a warrior, she now works as a hunter and gathers food.

She bears scars from a fight with a wolf.

## Rebuilding Skjeggestad

In the winter of 793–794, the Tjugeskalle people, led by Brynhild, were entrusted with rebuilding Skjeggestad when the weather allowed. The work was intended to strengthen Ravnskjær's position. She worked closely with Asgeir on hunting and rebuilding, and by the summer of 794 the area was beginning to be used again.

## Governing Skjeggestad

During the winter of 794–795, Brynhild governed Skjeggestad and worked with Asgeir on the hunting that supplied Ravnskjær with meat.
