Bjarte «Ulfbane» Berget is a gigantic warrior in the hird. Eadgifu is his partner.

## Joining the hird in 793

Bjarte joined the hird after its return from the first Lindisfarne voyage, replacing Kjartan Klippen. He had not taken part in that voyage.

When a wolf-creature killed a guard near Ravnskjær and attacked Finn Tjugeskalle, Bjarte brought it down with a blow that severed its head. The company called him «Ulvebane», Wolfbane, after this; the character list also uses the form «Ulfbane». He tried to save Finn, but failed. At the time, Bjarte had supported Finn politically.

Bjarte stayed in Ravnskjær when Jardar, Torstein, Trym, Asgeir and Saia later travelled to Skjeggestad.

## Eadgifu in Vatnaborg

On the second westward voyage in 793, Bjarte met Eadgifu in Vatnaborg and fell in love with her. After deaths struck the town, she chose to accompany the company. Upon their return, she moved in with him in Ravnskjær.
