Bjarte «Ulfbane» Berget is a gigantic warrior in the hird. Eadgifu is his partner.

## Joining the hird in 793

Bjarte joined the hird after its return from the first Lindisfarne voyage, replacing Kjartan Klippen. He had not taken part in that voyage.

When a wolf-creature killed a guard near Ravnskjær and attacked Finn Tjugeskalle, Bjarte brought it down with a blow that severed its head. The company called him «Ulvebane», Wolfbane, after this; the character list also uses the form «Ulfbane». He tried to save Finn, but failed. At the time, Bjarte had supported Finn politically.

Bjarte stayed in Ravnskjær when Jardar, Torstein, Trym, Asgeir and Saia later travelled to Skjeggestad.

## Eadgifu in Vatnaborg

On the second westward voyage in 793, Bjarte met Eadgifu in Vatnaborg and fell in love with her. After deaths struck the town, she chose to accompany the company. Upon their return, she moved in with him in Ravnskjær.

## Responsibility and jotunn blood

After the battle in Skardvik, the company heard a distant, guttural roar. Bjarte felt as though something had been torn loose or had answered back; the experience has not been further explained.

In the spring of 794 he became second-in-command of Jardar's watch. His bond with Eadgifu grew through spring and summer.

Before the scout warned of Eldrbrand’s arrival in the late summer of 794, Bjarte had already felt something in his body and understood that the jotunn was approaching.

During [the battle against Eldrbrand](/en/timeline/kampen-mot-eldrbrand), he travelled to the other side with Jardar and Trym. His jotunn blood answered the door of fire, and he led them into what Ben-Gerd called Muspelheim. At the altar towers, he unleashed his anger and smashed one of the towers. He was badly wounded fighting Eldrbrand's essence, but returned and received the antidote from Eadgifu.

## The guards and the bear in his blood

Later in 795, Bjarte took command of the guards within Jardar's overall defence. He discovered Geir Fieri and another guard murdered at the harbour, and the boats wrecked before the attack.

During the battle he killed an enormous fomor, a “Husk”, after it had smashed a gate. Later he tore the lower jaw off a werewolf chasing people towards the longhouse. Another attacker tore open half his throat.

When the box opened, he saw another forest overlaid upon Ravnskjær and a vast, shifting presence above it. He awakened as Gurahl, in bear form, and sensed the dead and wounded around him through the earth. He fought on beside Jardar and Torstein and drove the enemy away.
