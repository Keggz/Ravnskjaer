Njoldr had recently been promoted to captain of the guard when the attack on Ravnskjær came in 795. At Jardar's order he took command of the troops on the palisade.

When Rolf ran out against the enemy, Njoldr and Jardar descended from the palisade and the guards joined the battle. Njoldr was still with the defenders when they withdrew inside and formed a shield line. His subsequent fate is not recorded here.
