Vera «Brennesle» is Ragna's niece and belongs to the Torløv clan. She is recorded as being about fifteen or sixteen years old.

## Herbs and mixtures

Vera studies plants and alchemy. She can prepare herbal mixtures and healing compounds, and knows what can be mixed, eaten or should be avoided.
