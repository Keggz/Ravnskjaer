Hjalti Urdsson is level-headed and precise. He maps new trade routes and arranges barter agreements with smaller settlements on behalf of Åsmund Helgason.

He mostly works alone, but may travel with a small party along the paths and coast. He has good connections with Skjeggestad to the south.
