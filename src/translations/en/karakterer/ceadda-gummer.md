Ceadda Gummer is an elderly monk. He is listed among the three thralls brought back from Lindisfarne in 793. His earlier origins are unknown.

## Conversations with Halvdan

After being brought to Ravnskjær, Ceadda taught Halvdan Skrivare some English and Latin. This helped Halvdan discover the name Lindisfarne, which the travellers had not known during the expedition.
