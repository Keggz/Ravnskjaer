Solveig is Åskell Helgesson's daughter and is known as a healer and midwife. She was about thirty years old during the events in Skardvik.

She cared for Trym when he collapsed, and knew a ritual against spirits that possessed people. While the others defended the hall, she worked to free Tor.

When her father was mortally wounded, she sacrificed him and used his heart in the ritual. The attacking dead immediately collapsed, and Tor and Trym briefly awoke. Solveig survived the battle and accompanied the others to Ravnskjær.
