Ulrik the Little came from Hrafnvik north of Ravnskjær and was Alrik the Great’s twin brother. Both were chosen for the hird on the strength of the summer games after Eldrbrand’s attack in 794.

In 795 they accompanied Trym and Asgeir as protection on the journey with the box to Jotunheimen. The company was ambushed. Runa Måneveis remembered Trym and Asgeir dying but could not account for the twins. They were presumed dead, but this has not been confirmed.
