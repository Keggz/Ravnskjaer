Rolf Bakstein is Ravnskjær's only baker. He is jovial and talkative, and known for rising before everyone else to heat his stone oven.

## The boar by the palisade

During the council before the battle in 795, Rolf was strikingly eager to fight. When the attack came, he asked to be let through the gate and struck down two of the first enemies. The guards and defenders followed him outside.

Later they lost sight of him. When the fomori fled after Jardar, Torstein and Bjarte transformed, a boar-being stood outside the palisade striking them down. It proved to be Rolf. Shortly afterwards he returned to human form alongside the other three.
