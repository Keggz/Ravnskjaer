Eadgifu is Bjarte's partner. She is recorded as being about 30–35 years old.

Her family and earlier origins are not yet known. She lived in Vatnaborg when she met Bjarte.

## From Vatnaborg to Ravnskjær

Eadgifu met Bjarte during the company's first visit to Vatnaborg on the second westward voyage in 793. He promised to return before sailing home. After the deaths in the town, she chose to go with him, and moved in with Bjarte in Ravnskjær upon their return.
