Eadgifu is Bjarte's partner. She is recorded as being about 30–35 years old.

Her family and earlier origins are not yet known. She lived in Vatnaborg when she met Bjarte.

## From Vatnaborg to Ravnskjær

Eadgifu met Bjarte during the company's first visit to Vatnaborg on the second westward voyage in 793. He promised to return before sailing home. After the deaths in the town, she chose to go with him, and moved in with Bjarte in Ravnskjær upon their return.

## The antidote in Ben-Gerd's hut

During the battle against Eldrbrand in the late summer of 794, Eadgifu cared for Jardar, Trym and Bjarte in Ben-Gerd's hut while they fought on the other side. When they returned, she gave them the antidote to Ben-Gerd's poisonous brew. By then Ben-Gerd had been killed on the battlefield.

## Askebærerne

After Eldrbrand's attack, it emerged that Eadgifu also belonged to Askebærerne and that her journey to Ravnskjær had not been a matter of chance. She had no supernatural abilities, but an open mind and knowledge of alchemy and healing. Bjarte received the revelation well.

She helped clear the wreckage alongside Vilmar, the company and the survivors. She also said that Ben-Gerd's hut did not feel empty. What this meant has not been established.
