Geir Fieri came from Haugnes south of Ravnskjær and fought with a hand axe. He was among those chosen for the hird on the strength of the summer games after Eldrbrand’s attack in 794.

Before the battle for Ravnskjær in 795, Bjarte found him and another guard murdered at the harbour. The boats had been sabotaged, and the attackers were gone when Bjarte arrived.
