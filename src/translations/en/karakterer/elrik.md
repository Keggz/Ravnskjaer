Elrik is an adopted son of the Helga clan and comes from the East. He is described as quiet and dutiful.

He maintains ships and navigates alongside Hårek Stjernesyn.

## A larger boat

In the summer of 795, Elrik and his boatbuilders completed a new, larger boat. It had not yet been launched and awaited the next voyage west.
