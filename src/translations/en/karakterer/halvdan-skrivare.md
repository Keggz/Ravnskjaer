Halvdan Skrivare preserves important events, stories, legends and other cultural memories. He is described as clever and amusing.

## Keeper of history

He carves events, names and sayings into rune sticks, which he carries in a bundle at his hip. He works closely with Åsmund Helgason and serves as both recorder and keeper of Ravnskjær's history.

## The island's name

After the first westward voyage, Ceadda Gummer taught him some English and Latin. Through this work, Halvdan discovered the name Lindisfarne. The company had not known the island's name during their first visit.

## Writings from the west

Before the second westward voyage, Halvdan and Yrsa Åsmundsdatter worked on their English and the writings from the first raid. Progress was slow, and they hoped to have more ready by winter.

Throughout the winter of 793–794, Halvdan, Yrsa and Æthelberth were among those with whom Trym began to learn Latin. The notes do not establish that all the parchments had been translated.
