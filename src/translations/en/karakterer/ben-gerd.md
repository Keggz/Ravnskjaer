Ben-Gerd was known as a mysterious and eccentric witch. She never charged for her advice. Even the jarl's people sought her out quietly when grief or fear became too heavy to bear.

## How people remember her

No one knew exactly how old she was. Many children feared her, but people said she was rarely wrong, and that her advice saved more lives than swords did. She was known for hearing voices from the spirit world; some believed she could walk between dream and reality.

## A final act of protection

Ben-Gerd was crushed by Eldrbrand while protecting Asgeir from certain death.

## The journey to the other side

Before Eldrbrand's attack in the late summer of 794, Ben-Gerd prepared a poisonous brew and an antidote. Jardar, Trym and Bjarte drank the brew to reach the jotunn's essence on the other side, in what she called Muspelheim. She was sure they would arrive, although she could not locate the place precisely.

During the battle she threw Asgeir clear of Eldrbrand's hand and was seized herself. Those nearby seemed to witness a brief exchange between her and the jotunn before he killed her. The words are unknown. When the three travellers awoke in the hut, Eadgifu was caring for them and gave them the antidote.

## The connection to Askebærerne

In the conversations after the battle, it emerged that Ben-Gerd had worked closely with Askebærerne for a long time, while remaining something of her own. This does not establish that she was a member of the order. Eadgifu said that her hut did not feel empty; no definite explanation is known.
