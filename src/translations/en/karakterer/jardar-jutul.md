Jardar is a pragmatic warrior in the hird.

## Gyda and Steinvik

Gyda was Jardar's wife and is dead. When the company visited the burned ruins of Steinvik in the late summer or autumn of 793, Jardar sensed her presence. She spoke through Trym and swore to watch over him.

## The guards and the winter of 793–794

After the second westward voyage, Jardar accepted Bjørn's offer to lead Ravnskjær's guards and reserves. He was concerned with protecting the inhabitants, and particularly worried about Gunhild Treløse, whom he had rescued from Steinvik many years earlier.

During the voyage, Jardar was Asgeir's deputy at the helm, while Saia led the expedition. Beneath Stonehenge, the creature bit him in the neck, leaving two bite marks.

## The dream before the voyage

Before the first voyage, Jardar, Saia, Torstein and Asgeir shared a dream with slight variations. [Watch the four surviving versions](/en/dreams-and-omens/#drommen-for-ferden). It has not been established who received which version.

## Arle and Skardvik

During the attack on Skardvik's longhouse in the winter of 793–794, Jardar saw that Arle was in mortal danger after Bera and Hemming, the boy's parents, had been killed. He barely managed to save Arle and took him under his wing. They returned to Ravnskjær with the other survivors.
