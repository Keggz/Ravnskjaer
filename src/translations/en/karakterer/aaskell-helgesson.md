Åskell was a weary, proud leader, about fifty years old when the winter of 793–794 struck Skardvik. Bjørn Ulvsson considered him an ally and a man who did not ask for help lightly.

During the siege, Åskell gave a speech to keep the survivors' spirits up. He was mortally wounded when the barricades fell. His daughter Solveig called him over, embraced him and sacrificed his life in the ritual that freed Tor from Náðskrímr's grip. What father and daughter said to one another is not known.
