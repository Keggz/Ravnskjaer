Harald Hårmagre was an archer from Kvalnes north of Ravnskjær. He was chosen for the hird on the strength of the summer games after Eldrbrand’s attack in 794.

During the battle in 795 he stood at the front of the shield formation with Torstein, Jardar, Bjarte and Ulf Blodøye. The great werewolf attacking the line tore open his abdomen. The account then follows Torstein’s fight and does not establish Harald’s subsequent fate.
