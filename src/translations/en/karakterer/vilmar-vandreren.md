Vilmar appears languid and weary, yet also older than he ought to be. He carries a staff carved with a raven and wears a wolf-fur cloak. His eyes are ash-grey, with a flicker of blue.

## Rumours of the wanderer

Some say he came from the south; others believe he stepped out of the mist on Ravnskjær's shore one grey morning. No one saw his boat or knew him.

There are also rumours that, seen from certain angles, he casts no shadow.

## The meeting with Saia

Before the journey to Skjeggestad in 793, Vilmar met Saia on her way to Ben-Gerd. He appeared from behind a bush, earning him the company's nickname «the bush man».

He asked about the box, urged them to take good care of it and said he had been watching them. He also warned that their paths would cross again. Saia told the others about the meeting.

## The meeting in Vatnaborg

Trym met Vilmar again in Vatnaborg after the company's visit beneath Stonehenge in 793. The wanderer hinted at other ways of travelling and other travelling companions, but did not explain how he had arrived there. He asked the company to take care of what they had found and mentioned jötnar such as Eldrbrand, the destroyer of Steinvik.
