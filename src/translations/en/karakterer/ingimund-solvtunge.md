Ingimund is young, fair-haired and sociable, with fine clothes and a silver clasp that stands apart from the rest. He speaks readily, learns names quickly and finds his footing among strangers.

In 795 he performed a poem in competition with Åsleik Nefja, Kadlin Bjørnsdatter and Torbjørn Kvedulvsson. Jardar, Torstein and Bjarte chose him as the company's skald. His role was to travel with them, preserve their deeds and help in social encounters.
