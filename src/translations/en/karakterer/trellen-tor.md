Tor was sold to Ravnskjær from a passing merchant ship from the south. He works hard to secure his freedom.

During the summer games in 793, he took over after Egil den svake died in the boulder-lifting contest.

## The disappearance in 793

When the first expedition returned from Lindisfarne, the company learned that Tor had vanished while they were at sea. The notes do not explain his disappearance.

## Rumours from the north

Throughout the winter of 793–794, there were rumours that Tor had been seen farther north, in the Skardvik area. This was an unconfirmed sighting, not an explanation for his disappearance.

## The events in Skardvik

In the winter of 793–794, the company found Tor in Skardvik, naked and covered in ice in the blizzard, possessed by [Náðskrímr](/en/penumbra/spirits/nadskrimr). They captured him and carried him into the besieged longhouse.

Solveig Åskellsdatter sacrificed her mortally wounded father in a ritual to break the spirit's grip. Tor briefly awoke as the attacking dead collapsed. He survived and was taken back to Ravnskjær, but remained severely weakened for several nights after the return. How he first fell into the spirit's grip is still unclear.

## After returning home

Tor never quite returned to himself after Skardvik. He was there among the people, but seemed as though something within him was missing. What had been lost has not been established.
