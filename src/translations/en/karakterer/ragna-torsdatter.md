Ragna Torsdatter is a seeress and leader of the Torløv clan. She is solemn and profound, and is spoken of with both respect and a measure of fear.

## Family and teachings

Eidrid studies under her to learn to see and hear more than most people. Leiv is her son, and Vera «Brennesle» is her niece.
