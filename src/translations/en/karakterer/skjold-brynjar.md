Skjold-Brynjar rode into Steinvik with Runa Måneveis, Geir Ulvhjart and Yrsa Nattøyne in 793. By the fire, they spoke with the company from Ravnskjær about the box, the keys and the supernatural threats.

## The rescue during the ambush

During the ambush of the Jotunheimen company in 795, Skjold-Brynjar recovered the box and rescued Runa Måneveis from the fighting. He put her on a horse with the box so she could reach Ravnskjær. Runa's memories do not establish what happened to him afterwards.
