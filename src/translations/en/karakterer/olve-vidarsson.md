Olve Vidarsson was one of the four young boys who accompanied the westward expedition in 793. He nearly went overboard during the storm, but Asgeir Eriksson risked his own life and caught him.

At Lindisfarne, he stayed in camp with the other boys, Ragnhild Skjoldmøy and Sigurd Galeis.
