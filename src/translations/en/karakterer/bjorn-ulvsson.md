Bjørn Ulvsson was jarl of Ravnskjær and belonged to the Ulvsson clan. He is described as a proud and just leader.

## Family and legacy

Bjørn was married to Inga. Eirik Ravnblod is recorded as his firstborn son, and Solveig Bjørnsdatter as the daughter of Bjørn and Inga.

Bjørn died when the jötunn Eldrbrand attacked Ravnskjær. Torstein Bjørnson rules after him.

## The jarl's final battle

In the late summer of 794, Bjørn assembled the defence against Eldrbrand and had most inhabitants evacuated. He rallied the men after the first paralysing sight of the jotunn. When Ben-Gerd was killed, he led them into the attack for Ravnskjær.

Eldrbrand's boulder killed many defenders and destroyed half the longhouse. Bjørn was mortally wounded. Asgeir found him still alive inside the gate after the jotunn had been defeated. Bjørn met Asgeir with a questioning look and died after receiving a nod in reply.
