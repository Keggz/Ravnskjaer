## After the winter

Winter finally loosened its hold on Ravnskjær. Those who returned from Skardvik brought more than cold home with them. Tor walked among us again, but was never quite the same. It was as though something in him had gone missing. Jardar cared for Arle, teaching and protecting him like the son he had never had.

During the winter it had already been decided that the Tjugeskalle people, led by Brynhild, would rebuild Skjeggestad as soon as the weather allowed. Ravnskjær's influence was to grow where the company had left ashes. Asgeir and Brynhild worked closely together on hunting and rebuilding.

Early in spring the palisade was finished. Much of the woodland near Ravnskjær had supplied its timber. From the defences, the guards could now see farther across the land. Bjarte took on more responsibility as second-in-command of Jardar's watch, and his bond with Eadgifu grew stronger. Trym continued studying languages with Yrsa Åsmundsdatter and me.

## When summer came

Then summer came. Work at Skjeggestad had advanced far enough for the area to be used again. Tilde gave birth to the child she and Torstein had been awaiting. I have not yet entered the name here. We spoke of new voyages west, of the world beyond our coast and of what Ravnskjær might become.

It is strange how much future people can fit into a summer.
